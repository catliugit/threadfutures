How we use your details.

Updated Tue 24 Nov 2026.

Who we are

Thread Futures is an independent project by Cathy Liu, London. Not yet incorporated. Cathy Liu is the data controller.

Contact: via the form on our website.

What we collect

  • From the contact form: your name, work email, company and message.
  • Business contact details of people at brands, warehouses, trade buyers and insolvency firms that we find in public or professional sources: company websites, LinkedIn, Companies House, the Gazette and a contact-data provider.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use these details to reply to enquiries and to contact businesses about how unsold stock is handled. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. We do not sell data and do not send marketing to individual consumers.

Who processes it for us

  • Netlify: website hosting and form handling (United States).
  • Google: email and documents (Google Workspace).
  • Google Fonts: fonts on this site are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

International transfers rely on the UK–US data bridge or standard contractual clauses where the provider offers them.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted. Call recordings are made only with your consent and are deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to how we use it (including to any direct marketing, at any time). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.
